# Proposed English-Language Markets

Research date: September 20, 2026. Status: recommendation, NOT owner approval or
legal clearance. The owner requested 10-15 likely purchasing countries for an
English-language product. International-first remains the strategy; no payments
or real learner data are enabled by this document.

## Recommended Order

This is a qualitative prioritization of product fit, English-language reach,
purchasing-power proxies and available home-education evidence. It is NOT a
measured ranking of conversion rates, lifetime value or total app revenue.

| Priority | Country | Segment to validate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | United States | Homeschool and caregiver-led supplementary learning |
| 2 | United Kingdom | Home education and supplementary learning |
| 3 | Canada | English-speaking families; regional language needs remain separate |
| 4 | Australia | Home education and early learning |
| 5 | Ireland | Smaller English-language family-learning market |
| 6 | New Zealand | Smaller, documented home-education audience |
| 7 | Singapore | English-language enrichment, not assumed homeschool demand |
| 8 | United Arab Emirates | English-medium and international-school families |
| 9 | South Africa | English-speaking home-learning families |
| 10 | Malaysia | English-medium enrichment |
| 11 | India | English-speaking family segment; validate local pricing |
| 12 | Philippines | English-language learning; validate local pricing |

Prioritize the first six for release preparation. Validate the remaining six
before committing acquisition budgets. Pricing sensitivity, payment access,
acquisition costs and actual parent demand have NOT been measured. A large
population is not a forecast of paying customers. No market is approved yet.

## Evidence and Limits

- [World Bank country classifications](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/906519-world-bank-country-and-lending-groups)
  provide a coarse purchasing-power proxy. National income classifications do
  not measure a household's willingness to buy this app.
- [US NCES homeschooling surveys](https://nces.ed.gov/nhes/homeschooling.asp),
  [England's parliamentary briefing](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/sn05108/),
  [Statistics Canada](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3710017901)
  and [New Zealand Education Counts](https://www.educationcounts.govt.nz/statistics/homeschooling)
  document relevant audiences, not purchase intent. England's figures are not
  UK-wide. School-age homeschool counts do not size the toddler market.
- [Singapore Census 2020](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/-/media/files/publications/cop2020/sr1/cop2020sr1.ashx)
  documents English use at home. This is an older language-fit signal, not a
  current paid-app demand measurement.
- [Dubai KHDA's 2024-25 private-school report](https://web.khda.gov.ae/en/About-Us/News/2025/Dubais-private-school-sector-records-6-enrolment)
  documents a substantial UK/US curriculum presence. Dubai is not the whole
  UAE, and school enrolment does not imply interest in homeschooling.
- [EF's 2025 language reports](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/downloads/) can help
  investigate English-language segments. Their adult test-taker samples are not
  representative measures of children's proficiency or purchasing behavior.

## Before Any Country Goes Live

Confirm publisher/support ownership, approved age bands, country-specific
privacy/consumer/accessibility review, content and voice rights, English spelling
and pronunciation fit, store eligibility, hosting data practices and tax/payment
obligations if charging later. Do not imply curriculum accreditation. US and UK
English are content variants, not universal substitutes for regional review.

Conduct adult-only synthetic usability and price research until the pilot's
real-data approval gate is met. Assess affordability and conversion with actual
caregiver research before treating this shortlist as a sales plan.
